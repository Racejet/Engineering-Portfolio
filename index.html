About

My stream of interest lies in engineering where the implementation of design skills and individual and teamwork skills is extremely important:

  • Design - a solution/product that is planned out, prototyped and iterated multiple times until it meets all requirements and addresses relevant external factors to solve a problem
  • Individual and teamwork skills – an ability to perform effectively both independently and as a member of a team

This portfolio will highlight some of the project experiences, volunteer work and extracurricular involvements that have influenced me as an individual, team member and engineering student. They reflect my holistic approach to life and the design process and my aptitude for effective individual and teamwork – skills I plan to use to help improve people’s lives through engineering.
