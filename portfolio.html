    • Bipedal Walking Robots (2021)

    Objective

  • To address the limited/fixed step size of walking robot legs used in an interactive exhibition on mammal locomotion
  • To redesign bipedal walking robot legs that can be adjusted to enable different step sizes
    • Solution
  • Attached linear actuators that can extend, allowing various step sizes to be achieved
  • Utilised MATLAB to simulate the motions of the bipedal walking robot leg at different leg and step sizes
    • Design Process
  • Our team researched, coded, planned, and iterated on our design
  • I outlined and researched key objectives for design achievement
  • I also developed theoretical loop equations that helped develop the MATLAB code
  • Wrote and edited the position and velocity analysis parts of the final document
  • This design project allowed me to improve my MATLAB, research, communication and collaboration skills - skills that are transferable to future engineering design projects

    • DIY DC power supply (2022)

    Objective

  • To design and create a low-cost adjustable DC power supply that can produce an output voltage of 0-15V and an output current of 0-3A
  • Focus was creating a current sense amplifier and filter to connect to an Arduino Nano that can control the output voltage and current
    • Solution
  • Create a printed circuit board (PCB) with circuit designs for current sense amplifier and filter to control the power supply output
  • Solder the PCB together and put it together with other DC power supply components
    • Design Process
  • Our team created multiple circuit designs using PSPICE for a current sense amplifier and filter, and selected the most optimal
  • I Utilised EAGLE to create professional schematics and PCB layouts required in this power supply
  • I soldered on a majority components onto our PCBs and power supply
  • This project provided a learning opportunity in building a PCB, using software to build circuit schematics and soldering skills

    • Thermal Characterization and simulation framework of large-format electric vehicle lithium-ion batteries (2023-2024)

    Objective & Solution

  • Develop a testing rig that will improve results in thermal characterization of battery cells used for direct current charging in electric vehicles (EVs)
  • Simulate performance of the modular battery system in COMSOL utilizing experimental data from cell testing

    • Design Process
  • Our team researched current methods in characterizing cells, heat mitigation in battery cells and standards
  • Solidworks was utilised to layout the testing rig design and COMSOL was utilized for thermal characterization of the cells
  • I took charge in our group’s COMSOL simulation learning and simulation designs
  • Helped construct and prototype the cell testing rig
  • This design project helped improve and showcase my research, communication and collaboration skills
  • This project also has given me industry knowledge of EVs and battery cells
