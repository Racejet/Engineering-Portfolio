Silo App | Shad 2019

SHAD

  • An annual STEAM/entrepreneurship program that tasks participants with solving a specific social issue
    • Objective
  • To find a solution to reduce food waste
    • Solution
  • A computer app enabling consumers to purchase directly from farmers – this reduces food waste by decreasing the amount of food spoilage that occurs along the supply chain while goods are in storage and transit
    • Design Process
  • Participants were placed into teams to research, design and engineer a prototype
  • As chief researcher, my main responsibility involved studying the impact of food waste, the ways in which it is wasted and any solutions already in existence - I identified supply chain food wastage as an oftentimes overlooked factor – this became the focus for our solution
  • This project introduced me to my very first design process, allowing me to experience the power of individual and teamwork in a quest towards a common goal – this is important in engineering in which teamwork is as critical for success as individual work - our design reflected a holistic approach – this is also important in engineering in which consideration for the whole person, society and the ‘big picture’ is paramount

    • Ergonomic Shelving Design (2020)

    Project

  • The team was tasked with solving an ergonomic challenge faced by employees of the University of Toronto Engineering Store Redesign of the store’s shelving units while mindful of a low budget requiring the use primarily of pre-existing materials
    • Objective

  • To redesign the store’s shelving units while mindful of a low budget requiring the use primarily of pre-existing materials
    • Solution
  • A system utilizing magnets to hold up shelf covers, thereby providing employees with easier more ergonomic access to the store’s shelves
    • Design Process
  • As project manager I was responsible for team communications and the scheduling, organizing and co-ordinating of meetings and tasks
  • I set up our PowerPoint presentation slideshow and proposed and presented the project’s measures of success
  • This design project allowed me to assume a leadership role where utilizing and developing time and people management skills was very important as was being accountable while also ensuring the accountability of others – this ability to work well alone and with others and to assume a leadership role when required is important in engineering which involves a multidisciplinary approach
