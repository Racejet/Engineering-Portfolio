Silo App | Shad (2019)

Silo App | Shad (2019)

SHAD

  • An annual STEAM/entrepreneurship program that tasks participants with solving a specific social issue
    • Objective
  • To find a solution to reduce food waste
    • Solution
  • A computer app enabling consumers to purchase directly from farmers – this reduces food waste by decreasing the amount of food spoilage that occurs along the supply chain while goods are in storage and transit
    • Design Process
  • Participants were placed into teams to research, design and engineer a prototype
  • As chief researcher, my main responsibility involved studying the impact of food waste, the ways in which it is wasted and any solutions already in existence - I identified supply chain food wastage as an oftentimes overlooked factor – this became the focus for our solution
  • This project introduced me to my very first design process, allowing me to experience the power of individual and teamwork in a quest towards a common goal – this is important in engineering in which teamwork is as critical for success as individual work - our design reflected a holistic approach – this is also important in engineering in which consideration for the whole person, society and the ‘big picture’ is paramount

    • Ergonomic Shelving Design (2020)

    Project

  • The team was tasked with solving an ergonomic challenge faced by employees of the University of Toronto Engineering Store Redesign of the store’s shelving units while mindful of a low budget requiring the use primarily of pre-existing materials
    • Objective
  • To redesign the store’s shelving units while mindful of a low budget requiring the use primarily of pre-existing materials
    • Solution
  • A system utilizing magnets to hold up shelf covers, thereby providing employees with easier more ergonomic access to the store’s shelves
    • Design Process
  • As project manager I was responsible for team communications and the scheduling, organizing and co-ordinating of meetings and tasks
  • I set up our PowerPoint presentation slideshow and proposed and presented the project’s measures of success
  • This design project allowed me to assume a leadership role where utilizing and developing time and people management skills was very important as was being accountable while also ensuring the accountability of others – this ability to work well alone and with others and to assume a leadership role when required is important in engineering which involves a multidisciplinary approach

    • 3D Printer Design (2020)

    Objective

  • To address the inefficiencies of current 3D printers
  • Design a 3D Printer that simultaneously achieves a high extrusion rate, low error and an optimal filament to air ratio all at once
    • Solution
  • Utilised Solidworks to design a 3D Printer that maximizes print speed without compromising accuracy and strength of print
    • Design Process
  • Our team successfully researched, compared, planned, prototyped and iterated a 3D printer
  • Using CAD and Solidworks, I designed and prototyped printer parts
  • I prepared and wrote key parts of our team’s final document
  • As project manager I also organized team communications and the scheduling, organizing and co-ordinating of meetings and tasks
  • The CAD and Solidworks skills I acquired will be useful in my careers - the leadership, communication and collaboration skills important for the success of this particular project are skills transferable to future engineering design projects

    • Bipedal Walking Robots (2021)

    Objective

  • To address the limited/fixed step size of walking robot legs used in an interactive exhibition on mammal locomotion
  • To redesign bipedal walking robot legs that can be adjusted to enable different step sizes
    • Solution
  • Attached linear actuators that can extend, allowing various step sizes to be achieved
  • Utilised MATLAB to simulate the motions of the bipedal walking robot leg at different leg and step sizes
    • Design Process
  • Our team researched, coded, planned, and iterated on our design
  • I outlined and researched key objectives for design achievement
  • I also developed theoretical loop equations that helped develop the MATLAB code
  • Wrote and edited the position and velocity analysis parts of the final document
  • This design project allowed me to improve my MATLAB, research, communication and collaboration skills - skills that are transferable to future engineering design projects

    • DIY DC power supply (2022)

    Objective

  • To design and create a low-cost adjustable DC power supply that can produce an output voltage of 0-15V and an output current of 0-3A
  • Focus was creating a current sense amplifier and filter to connect to an Arduino Nano that can control the output voltage and current
    • Solution
  • Create a printed circuit board (PCB) with circuit designs for current sense amplifier and filter to control the power supply output
  • Solder the PCB together and put it together with other DC power supply components
    • Design Process
  • Our team created multiple circuit designs using PSPICE for a current sense amplifier and filter, and selected the most optimal
  • I Utilised EAGLE to create professional schematics and PCB layouts required in this power supply
  • I soldered on a majority components onto our PCBs and power supply
  • This project provided a learning opportunity in building a PCB, using software to build circuit schematics and soldering skills

    • Thermal Characterization and simulation framework of large-format electric vehicle lithium-ion batteries (2023-2024)

    Objective & Solution

  • Develop a testing rig that will improve results in thermal characterization of battery cells used for direct current charging in electric vehicles (EVs)
  • Simulate performance of the modular battery system in COMSOL utilizing experimental data from cell testing
  • Create possible solutions in COMSOL to help mitigate heat from the batteries
    • Design Process
  • Our team researched current methods in characterizing cells, heat mitigation in battery cells and standards
  • Solidworks was utilised to layout the testing rig design and COMSOL was utilized for thermal characterization of the cells
  • I took charge in our group’s COMSOL simulation learning and simulation designs
  • Helped construct and prototype the cell testing rig
  • This design project helped improve and showcase my research, communication and collaboration skills
  • This project also has given me industry knowledge of EVs and battery cells
